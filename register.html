Registration FAQ

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), PayPal, and UPI.

Are there group discounts available?

Yes! Groups of 5 or more receive an automatic 15% discount at checkout. Contact support for larger university groups.

Can I modify my registration later?

You can update your personal details or transfer your ticket to another person up to 7 days before the event starts.
